Home/About

A small company that acts like a large one.

Founded in 2014 with three cars and one dispatcher. Still owner-run, still answering the phone, now moving several thousand passengers a month across the Bay Area.

The premise

Most ground transportation is sold on the car. We think it is sold on the fifteen minutes after landing, when nothing has gone wrong.

Our story

Built by a dispatcher, not a marketing team.

Northlane started because our founder spent a decade watching good trips fail at the last mile: a driver at the wrong terminal, a fare that doubled in traffic, a phone that rang out at 11pm.

We built the company around the three things that actually break — arrival timing, price certainty, and the ability to reach a person. Everything else, including the cars, follows from those.

Today the fleet runs from a single operations base near the Embarcadero, with airport desks at SFO, OAK, and SJC, and a dispatch desk that never closes.

How we hire

Four checks before anyone drives.

Chauffeurs are the product. We hire slowly, train deliberately, and remove drivers who cut corners.

01

Background

Live Scan fingerprinting, DMV record, and a ten-year employment history verified before a first interview.

02

Driving assessment

A two-hour road assessment with a senior chauffeur, including airport terminal routes and defensive driving.

03

Service training

Meeting points, name boards, discretion, accessibility, and what to do when a passenger's plans change.

04

Ride-alongs

Ten supervised trips before solo assignment, then a review at 30 and 90 days with rider feedback.

05

Annual renewal

Defensive driving refresher, first aid, and a clean record check every year. No exceptions, including for tenure.

06

Direct accountability

Every ride is traceable to a named chauffeur. Complaints go to the operations lead, not a ticket queue.

Standards

The things we will not compromise.

These are operating commitments, not marketing lines. They are written into the dispatch manual and audited monthly.

  • A human answers the reservations line within three rings, 24 hours a day.
  • Every quoted fare is fixed at booking, including tolls and airport access.
  • If a car is more than fifteen minutes late for a scheduled pick-up, the ride is on us.
  • Passenger details are never sold, and card data is never stored by the booking engine.
  • Vehicles are replaced at four years, regardless of mileage, and maintained on a factory schedule.

Ride with us once. You will know.

The first booking takes a minute. The standard is the same whether you book one transfer or an account.
