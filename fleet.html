Access and extras

Small things, handled before you ask.

Child seats, extra stops, and accessible vehicles are arranged at booking so the driver arrives prepared.

  • Child and booster seats, fitted and inspected, at no extra charge.
  • Wheelchair-accessible vans available by phone with 24 hours' notice.
  • Water, chargers for every seat, and a quiet cabin as standard.
  • Extra stops and waiting time added at the quoted hourly rate, never as a surprise.
  • Electric and hybrid options on request for city transfers.
